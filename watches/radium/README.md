# The Radium Girls' pictures

Each picture keeps its own licence (`README.md` at the repo root, "License"). Each is
at the size of its source, and its metadata is removed. The Questions slide in
`slides.md` credits them.

- `painters.jpg`: [Dial painters at the United States Radium Corporation](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:All_women_or_girls_using_radium_paint_with_no_protection_or_warnings_in_1922,_from-_USRadiumGirls-Argonne1,ca1922-23-150dpi_(cropped).jpg>), unknown photographer, c. 1922, from the Rutgers University Libraries. Public domain.
- `fryer.jpg`: [Grace Fryer](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Grace_Fryer.jpg), unknown photographer, 1925. Public domain.
