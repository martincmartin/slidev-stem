# The Waltham Watch Factory's picture

Each picture keeps its own licence (`README.md` at the repo root, "License").
The Questions slide in `slides.md` credits it.

- `machine-room.jpg`: the Wheel and Pinion Making Room of the American Watch
  Company, Waltham, an albumen print by Thomas R. Lewis, c. 1874–79, on a book
  page that the Rijksmuseum scanned (RP-F-2001-7-980-60):
  [Commons](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gezicht_op_een_ruimte_van_de_American_Watch_Company_in_Waltham,_Massachusetts,_waar_tandwielen_worden_gemaakt_Pinion_Finishing_Room,_Watch_Factory_(titel_op_object),_RP-F-2001-7-980-60.jpg>).
  Public domain; the scan is CC0. It is the lower of the two photos on the
  page, cropped to the photo (1473 × 1097 px at 3534, 2035 of the 5784 × 4002
  px scan). Its contrast is stretched: in CIELAB, the darkest and the
  brightest 0.5% of the lightness go to black and white, and the colour
  channels keep the print's sepia. Then it is scaled to 1080 px wide, with
  its metadata removed (JPEG quality 85). The print itself is faded, with
  only about 96 of 256 grey levels. Not an oversight: a CSS `contrast()`
  filter pivots on mid-grey, so it turns the light ceiling under the title
  white first. The commands and the other variants are in
  `tasks/2026-11-08-waltham/evidence/`.
