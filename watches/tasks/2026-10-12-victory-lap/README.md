# The victory lap

A slide whose only text is the time scale: a tour of the train, from the
motion works to the balance, with a camera that tilts, turns and zooms. The options, the
evidence and the steps are in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md).

- `tools/touches.py`: where each touch is, the speed of each stop, and what
  a low view of the barrel's touch can see. Output: `out/touches.txt`.
- `tools/heights.ts`: each piece's heights in the model. Output:
  `out/heights.txt`.
- `tools/design-before.json`: the design at the start of the task.
