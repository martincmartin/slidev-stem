There are three problems here:

- The pallet stones area.  The pallet stones are what block the escape wheel from turning, so during each of "tick" and "tock", one side of the appropriate pallet stone should be flush against a tooth of the escape wheel.  At rest, the pallet stones shouldn't overlap the escape wheel at all.  Ideally, the escape wheel doesn't start moving until the pallet stone clears the tooth, and they never overlap, but honestly nobody is going to notice a brief overlap, or the wheel starting to move early/late, during motion.  The main point is that, when mesh works are still, it looks like the pallet stones are blocking the escape wheel teeth.

- Where the balance wheel's arms connect with the balance wheel, there's a line.  Because the balance wheel has no markings that move along with it, like the teeth on the other wheels, its not clear that the annulus is moving.  Removing the lines helps the viewer realize it's all one part.  If that means making the arms the same width as the wheel, I'm fine with that.

- The hairspring's inner end floats. It should attach to the center.

- The hairspring doesn't maintain a constant length.  When it "curls up", the outsides actually expand instead of contract.  Change it so that it has a constant length: when curling up, the outside diameter should go down, and vice versa when it uncurls.
