# The Harrison portrait

The John Harrison slide's painting was dark and had a red cast. This task
found a better scan, chose how to lift its dark tones, and placed it at the
slide's full height, flush left.

- `instructions.md`: the request.
- `PLAN.md`: the scans on Commons, why the Waltham slide's contrast stretch
  does not fit, seven variants with their tones (histogram equalization, the
  flattest-histogram gamma, CLAHE), the choice, and the place on the slide.
- `evidence/`: a contact sheet of the four Commons scans, a contact sheet of
  the variants, and the script that makes and measures the variants
  (`variants.sh`, with `tones.py`).
