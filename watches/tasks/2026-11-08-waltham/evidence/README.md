# The candidate pictures

`candidates.jpg` is a contact sheet of the ten pictures that `PLAN.md`,
section 7, compares. It was made on 2026-11-08 with ImageMagick's `montage`.
Each picture is scaled down, numbered and labelled, and the sheet's metadata
is removed. Pictures 1 and 2 are cropped from one scanned book page. Each
picture keeps its own licence (`README.md` at the repo root, "License").

`contrast-variants.jpg` and `contrast-three.jpg` show six ways to give
picture 1 more contrast, cropped as the slide shows it (`PLAN.md`, section
13). `contrast-variants.sh` makes them from the Rijksmuseum's full scan.
