# The Waltham Watch Company

A 60-second section after the Harrison story and before the Radium Girls:
what to show, what to say, and the designs to choose from. Section 12 of
`PLAN.md` gives the slide as built.

- `instructions.md`: the request.
- `PLAN.md`: the time budget, your three points against the sources, what a
  picture can carry, seven designs with their counts, the ranking, a draft of
  the words, the pictures, the facts with their sources, and the decisions
  for you. Section 11, round 2, adds the evidence that the watches were good,
  and the 1853 railroad crash that follows the slide.
- `evidence/`: a contact sheet of the ten candidate pictures, and their
  sources and licences; two contact sheets of the photo's contrast variants,
  and the script that makes them (section 13).
