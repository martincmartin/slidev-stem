# The dial painter's photo

The Lip, Dip, Paint slide showed USRC's dial painters, about 1922, in a wide
shot of the room. On the user's request, it now shows a close-up of a dial
painter at a British clock factory, January 1932. This task found what the
photo shows, the best scan of it, and its crop.

- `instructions.md`: the requests, and the first answer.
- `PLAN.md`: what the photo is, Britain's dial painters, the photos compared
  for the slide, the three scans, the crop, and the rights.
- `evidence/`: the three scans side by side (`sources.jpg`), three crops at the
  slide's size (`crops.jpg`), and the script that makes both and
  `public/radium/painters.jpg` (`crop.sh`).
