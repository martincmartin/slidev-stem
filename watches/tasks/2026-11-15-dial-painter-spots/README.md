# The dust on the dial painter's photo

The scan of the 1932 negative on the Lip, Dip, Paint slide had black specks of
dust: one on her chin, and a cluster on the wall in front of her. This task
removed 14 specks by filling each from the pixels around it.

- `instructions.md`: the requests.
- `PLAN.md`: the specks, and the options (fill, lift from the Commons copy or
  from the print, or a service).
- `evidence/`: the photo before (`painters-before.jpg`, as the photo's task's
  `crop.sh` makes it), the script that makes `public/radium/painters.jpg` from it
  (`retouch.py`), and each speck before and after (`comparison.png`).
