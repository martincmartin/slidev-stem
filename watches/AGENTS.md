# What Every Nerd Should Know About Watches

The house rules for every STEM talk are in `../AGENTS.md` and `../docs/`. This
talk's own docs are in `docs/`.

## Docs: read before you act

Before you…

- add or change a slide: `docs/slide-patterns.md`.
- change the watch (`watch/`, `art/`, its frontmatter, `global-bottom.vue`) or
  the diving board: `docs/watch.md`, and `../docs/webgl.md` for `webgl/`.
- write or change a slide's narration, or change the article
  (`addons/narration/`, `components/FigureLayer.vue`, `watch/article.ts`),
  build it or export its text: `docs/article.md`, and `../docs/export.md`.
- change a drawing, or claim that one is legible: `docs/measurements.md`,
  `../docs/legibility.md`.
- choose what the next slide says: `docs/rough-slides.md`.
- update packages or remove a dependency override: `docs/packages.md`, and
  `../docs/talks.md`.
- sort a correction, or tag the talk: `docs/watchlist.md`, with the
  `learn-from-corrections` skill.
