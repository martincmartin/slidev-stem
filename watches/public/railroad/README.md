# The railroad crash's picture

Each picture keeps its own licence (`README.md` at the repo root, "License").
The Questions slide in `slides.md` credits it.

- `valley-falls-1853.jpg`: the head-on collision of two Providence &
  Worcester trains near Valley Falls, Rhode Island, August 12, 1853, a
  half-plate daguerreotype attributed to L. Wright of Pawtucket, at the George
  Eastman Museum, from the Google Art Project:
  [Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:L._Wright_-_Train_wreck_on_the_Providence_Worcester_Railroad_near_Pawtucket,_Rhode_Island_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg).
  Public domain. The plate is oval, x 65–3712 and y 100–2795 of the
  3791 × 2878 px scan, with a dark rim of tarnish about 100 px wide inside
  its edge. The picture is a crop of the slide's shape, 2465 × 1386 px at
  528, 533. It is 10% smaller than the largest crop, centred on the oval,
  that stays clear of the rim (2720 × 1530 px at 528, 683), so that less of
  the ground shows, and it cuts the car at the right in half. It is 150 px
  higher, so that the text at the right is in the sky, above the car's roof;
  a little tarnish shows in its top corners.
  Then it is scaled to 1470 px wide, with its metadata removed (JPEG
  quality 82).
  The crops are compared in `tasks/2026-11-08-railroad/evidence/`.
